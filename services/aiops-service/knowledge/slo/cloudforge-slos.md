# CloudForge AI SLOs

## Evaluation window

The current SLO evaluation window is 7 days.

## Availability SLO

Target:

99.9% availability.

## Request Success SLO

Target:

99% successful requests.

## P95 Latency SLO

Target:

95% of requests should complete below 500 milliseconds.

## Error budget

Error budget represents the amount of unreliability permitted while remaining within the SLO target.

## Burn rate

Burn rate describes how quickly an error budget is being consumed relative to the expected rate.

## AI interpretation

AI may explain SLO state and operational evidence.

AI must not alter SLO definitions or declare an SLO healthy or breached using fabricated telemetry.

Deterministic SLO calculations remain authoritative.
